## Arriving

1. **A small village from the strategic map.** Select a village and Enter.
   - You stand on its road just past a timber gateway, facing into the village.
   - You are not out in the fields.
2. **An ordinary town.** You stand just inside its front gate, on the road, facing the streets, with the gatehouse behind you.
3. **A large or fortified settlement** (a city or fortress, if your world has one). The same; its gatehouse is larger and taller.
   - *Not seen in the test world: please look closely.*
4. **The front gate.** Turn round. Is this the gate you would call the main one, on the road you would expect?
5. **Walk straight in and out** through the gate, both ways. Nothing catches you: not the leaves, not the portcullis, not the lanterns.

## The gate and the walls

6. **Gate and wall.** From outside, look along the wall either side of the gate.
   - The wall meets the towers with no gap.
   - Where a lane crosses the wall, there is a narrow opening with a stone lintel.
   - Nothing floats; the towers stand on the ground even on a slope.
7. **Guards at the gate.** By day, a guard stands beside the road on each side, just inside, looking out of the gate.
   - They are not in the road and not bunched together.
   - At night the main gate is still held.
8. **Guards on patrol.** Others walk between the gates and the square or the hall.
9. **Colours.** Guards wear the owner's colour, and the banners at the gate match the strategic map. Compare towns of different owners.

## Roads

10. **The approach.** Leave by the gate and follow the road out, then come back. The road leads straight to the gate and through it.
11. **Up and down hill.** Walk an approach road on a slope. Does the paving still look too angular to you where it climbs? (The ground itself was not reshaped: see the report.)
12. **Road ends and junctions** near the gate and in the town: no gaps and no sharp, square ends.

## In and out

13. **Leave.**
    - "Out to the country": you stand on the road outside, near the gate.
    - "Return to World Map": you are back on the map.
14. **Come back**, from the map and on foot from the country. Both times, the same front gate.
15. **The local map** (bottom right) right after arriving shows you at the gate, not where you were before.
16. **Life goes on.** The streets, shops, people, children and animals are as they were in Phase 27.
17. **Performance.** Entering a busy town and walking through the gate feels smooth.
