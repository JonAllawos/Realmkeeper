**Start a new world** to see new interiors everywhere. In an older save, interiors already entered keep their layout.

## Population and life

1. **Walk into a large town at midday.** The streets near the square are busy (dozens of people), the lanes beyond quieter.
   - People walk to and from doors, the market and the gates.
   - Some stand talking in the square; some work at workshop doors.
   - The watch stands at the gates and walks between the gates and the hall.
2. **Wait until evening and night (T).** Fewer people each hour; by 22:00 only a few, and a reduced watch.
3. **Visit a village and a hamlet.** Fewer people, in proportion to their size.
4. **Look at the people.** Varied looks and dress (labourers, farmers, smiths, elders); nobody in your way (they never block you); they cannot be talked to (only named residents can).
5. **Shopkeepers.** Walk past an open shop whose keeper is out or at the counter. Now and then they call a line of their trade. Not constantly, not two at once, never while a window or conversation is open. Calling never opens the shop.
6. **Animals.** In a pasture, chickens, cows and horses are kit models that graze about and never leave the fence. (Sheep and goats are still the old box figures.)

## Streets and buildings

7. **Signs.** A smithy, provisioner, apothecary, bowyer and the tavern show their trade's sign. Is each readable from the street? (This is not verified: please say.)
8. **Banners.** At the gates and by the hall, keep and guard posts, the owner's banner in their map colour.
9. **Dressing.** Anvil and bench by the smithy, barrels and crates by shops, benches round the square, carts, hay, wood piles. Nothing blocks a door or a road.
10. **Doors.** Walk to several houses: the path and the people go to the door itself, and the door fills its frame (no gaps at the sides). Are the tops right? (The height is unverified.)
11. **Variety.** Large houses, inns, smithies and warehouses look like what they are.

## Interiors

12. **Enter a cottage, a large house, a warehouse and a workshop.** Small buildings are small inside, large ones larger, with more rooms (a large house has a second bedroom) and furniture for the purpose (shelves and barrels in a store; tables, chairs and a rug at home).
13. **The tavern and a smithy** (open to the street): their halls and stations as before.

## Roads

14. **Corners and ends.** Corners are rounded, and road ends are rounded, not square.
15. **Hills.** Walk a road up and down a hill. The paving stays on the ground. Is any angle still too sharp to you?

## General

16. **A busy town** at midday: is it smooth enough? (Editor measurements only so far.)
17. **Save and reload** in town: the same people and dressing come back (they are derived from the same day and hour).
