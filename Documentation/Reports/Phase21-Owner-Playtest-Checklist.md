# Phase 21: Owner playtest checklist

Phase 21 was verified by automated tests and captured renders only. **Nobody has hand-played it.** Each item below says what to do and what you should see. Tick it, or note what you saw instead.

Start a **new game** for the specialist shops. An older save gains them too, the first time it loads.

## Weather and seasons

- [ ] **The clock shows the weather.** In a town, the HUD clock reads like "Light rain, 9 °C · Spring". Hover over it: you see the region's weather, wind, sunrise and sunset, what the weather changes, today's outlook, and the climate.
- [ ] **Rain looks right.** In rain the streets darken and gloss, haze closes in, rain falls around you and you hear it.
- [ ] **Storms.**
  - The sky goes dark grey and lamps come on early.
  - Lightning flashes every 7–22 seconds, with thunder after it.
  - People who work at the market or square go home until the period passes.
- [ ] **Snow.** In winter, snow falls in the cold regions. Lying snow whitens the ground and the roofs, and the grass is no longer green.
- [ ] **Fog.** Autumn and lakeland mornings can be foggy: the haze is thick and pale.
- [ ] **Clear nights.** Stars and a moon show; the sky is deep blue, not black.
- [ ] **Day length changes with the season.** Wait for evening in midsummer (Highsun) and in midwinter (Longnight):
  - in summer it is still light at 19:30;
  - in winter it is dark by about 16:50.
- [ ] **Bad weather slows travel.** On the atlas, travel an hour in heavy rain or snow. The line says "slowed by heavy rain" (or snow, or "in the pass"), and less ground is covered.
- [ ] **Saving keeps the weather.** Save in rain and load: the same rain, and tomorrow's weather follows on the same way.

## Shops

- [ ] **A town has its specialists.** It has, besides its outfitters, smithy, tavern and granary yard, specialists in its other shop buildings:
  - a leatherworks;
  - a bakery & provisions;
  - a tailor;
  - an apothecary;
  - possibly a bowyer & fletcher and a carpentry.

  Each is kept by a named townsperson.
- [ ] **Each sells only its trade.** In the trade window, the leatherworks sells leather goods and hides, the tailor clothes and cloth, the apothecary potions and herbs.
- [ ] **The trade window explains itself.** Under the ledger:
  - what the shop is;
  - what it has run out of, and why (for example "no trade road … is open to bring it in", or "the iron in … stores has run out");
  - the latest stock that came in, and from where. Hover for the full record.
- [ ] **Interiors match the trade.** Walk into each shop and turn to face the door:
  - **smithy:** forge with fire, anvil, quench barrel, weapon stand;
  - **leatherworks:** bench, peg rack, tanning tub;
  - **tailor:** cutting table, dress form;
  - **apothecary:** still over a fire, bottle shelf, bookcase;
  - **bakery:** brick oven with fire, apples, carrots.
- [ ] **A cut-off town runs down.** Suspend a town's trade routes (the inspector). Over a few days its shops stop restocking bought-in goods, and the shortfall says why.

## Stations and crafting

- [ ] **Working at a station.** In the smithy, interact with the forge corner ("Work at the forge and anvil"). The Hub opens on Crafting & work with a note naming the smithy.
- [ ] **Forging works while the shop is open.** With iron bars, a timber plank and smith's tools, start "Forge a shortsword": it starts.
- [ ] **Forging is refused while the shop is shut.** Try again after closing time: you are told the forge is in that shop and why it can't be used.
- [ ] **The new recipes are listed** under their disciplines, each with its station: bracers, leggings, riding boots, a backpack, a tunic, a padded coat, a hunting bow, arrows, a round shield, bread, rations, a shortsword, a hand axe, an iron cap and a chain shirt.
- [ ] **Keepers make goods.** Advance a few days and look at a leatherworks' latest supplies: "made at the leatherworker's bench by … from 1 cured leather".

## Interiors and people

- [ ] **Homes are furnished.** Enter a home, a large home, a workshop, the guard post and the warehouse:
  - beds in bedrooms;
  - a table and chairs in the hall;
  - barrels and crates in stores;
  - benches in workshops.

  No doorway is blocked and there is a warm light in each room.
- [ ] **Furniture stays put.** Leave and enter again: the furniture is in the same places.
- [ ] **People run errands.** On a rest day, between 13:00 and 16:30, people are at the town's shops (inspect them: "An errand at …").

## Saves

- [ ] **A Phase 20A save loads.** It has weather at once; its shops keep their stock and keepers; the town gains its specialists; and saving and loading again changes nothing.
