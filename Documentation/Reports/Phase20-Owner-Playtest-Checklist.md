# Phase 20: Owner playtest checklist (characters)

**Automated vs manual.** The automated tests click the real creator, sheet, conversation and party controls, and check the data. They cannot judge how things look or feel, how the camera handles, or how it reads at your resolution. Only you can.

**Set-up:**
1. Start a new world from the title screen (Menu ▸ New world).
2. Enter a town (select it, then Enter settlement).
3. Press **C**.

## A. Creator flow and layout
1. Readability:
   - [ ] Is the step bar readable?
   - [ ] Can you tell which steps are done?
2. The preview:
   - [ ] Is it large enough?
   - [ ] Is it well lit and framed?
   - [ ] Does dragging turn the model smoothly?
   - [ ] Does the wheel zoom?
3. Change frame, hair, skin tone, height and build:
   - [ ] Each shows on the model at once.
   - [ ] Height and build look plausible: not stretched, not too wide.
4. Choose **Tiefling**, then each of the three legacies:
   - [ ] The build panel lists the legacy until it is chosen.
   - [ ] The skin swatches now include ember, wine and violet.
5. Choose **Dragonborn**:
   - [ ] A note says the skin was set to a scale colour.
   - [ ] The beard option disappears.
6. On the Class step:
   - [ ] The subclass and "the road ahead" are useful.
   - [ ] The text fits.
7. On the right panel:
   - [ ] Hover each ability tile: the tooltip explains the number.
   - [ ] "Still to choose" matches what is missing.
8. Resize the window, or set Interface scale 130 %:
   - [ ] Below about 1380 px the build panel hides and the summary appears under the preview.
   - [ ] Nothing clips or overlaps.
9. Keyboard:
   - [ ] Tab between the identity fields.
   - [ ] Typing in a field does not move the character.
10. Press **Later (Esc)** half-way, then open the creator again:
    - [ ] Nothing was created.

## B. Character sheet
11. Create the character, then press **H** ▸ Character:
    - [ ] The name, concept and pronouns read well.
    - [ ] The ability tiles show score, modifier and save.
12. Look at the tooltips and the other tabs:
    - [ ] Every number explains itself on hover.
    - [ ] The **Origins** tab names your lineage and flags what the game cannot play yet.
13. Progression and Identity:
    - [ ] The **Progression** tab is clear.
    - [ ] **Identity** lets you edit your history.

## C. Level-up
14. Earn experience by playing, or through the DM tools on the map. Then press **L**:
    - [ ] The window explains the new level.
    - [ ] Closing it applies nothing.
    - [ ] Confirming applies everything once.
    - [ ] The Progression tab records it.

## D. Equipment and appearance
15. In Inventory, equip and unequip a helmet, armour, a shield and a weapon:
    - [ ] The preview and the body in the world change.
    - [ ] Your skin tone, height and build stay as chosen.

## E. People and companions
16. Talk to a stranger, then press **About them**:
    - [ ] Only what you have seen and been told is shown.
    - [ ] No numbers appear.
17. Talk with someone over days, until you know them well:
    - [ ] Their training and skills appear.
18. Recruit a companion (Travel with me), then open **H ▸ Party** and select them:
    - [ ] Ability tiles, saves, skills, features and loyalty are shown.
    - [ ] "About them" now shows their full measure.

## F. Save and load
19. Save (F5), quit to the title, then Continue:
    - [ ] Pronouns, concept, lineage, skin, height and build are all as you left them.
20. Load a world saved before Phase 20 that has a tiefling, goliath or gnome:
    - [ ] It keeps its old traits: fire resistance, Stone's Endurance, or tinker's tools.

## G. Overall
- [ ] Does the creator feel like a proper RPG character creator, not a form?
- [ ] Is it clear that non-human species use a placeholder body?

**Report** anything misleading or hard to read, with the step and the resolution.
