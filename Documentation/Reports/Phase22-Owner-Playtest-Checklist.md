# Phase 22: Owner playtest checklist

Nobody has hand-played Phase 22. These are the checks only a person at the keyboard can make. Each says what to do and what you should see. Tick what holds; note anything that does not.

## Character creation

1. **No experience before creation.**
   - Start a new world, enter a town, and do not create a character yet.
   - Finish anything that would pay experience (a quest, a discovery).
   - You should get no experience. Once created, the character has **0 experience** and no "Level up" button.
2. **The tiefling's choices.**
   - In the creator, choose **Tiefling** and a legacy.
   - Under the legacy, a **Legacy Spellcasting Ability** choice (Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) must be made before Next is allowed.
   - **Legacy Spell (level 3)** and **(level 5)** are listed with "(from level 3/5)" and "Locked: arrives at level N, not at creation".
3. **The review page.** Choose a caster (a wizard) and open **Review**. Check:
   - **Level** reads "1 · 0 experience (level 2 at 300) · proficiency +2";
   - **Armour class** matches the armour your kit puts on (a fighter in chain mail should show it);
   - **Level 1 features** lists nothing from level 2 or later;
   - **Magic** lists your cantrips and spells once each, "slots: 2 × 1st" (a warlock: one pact slot), and your save DC and attack;
   - **Uses** lists hit dice 1 and the class's level 1 uses;
   - **Later, from your species** names any later trait.
4. **Three sizes.** Repeat step 3 with the game window at **1920×1080**, **1366×768** and **1280×720**. Nothing should clip or overlap, and the Create button should stay visible.
5. **In the world.** After creating, open the sheet (H). You should see:
   - level 1, the same hit points and AC as the review, and nothing new;
   - the body with the chosen height, build, skin and eyes, as in the preview (the Phase 20A fix).

## Levelling up

6. **The notice.**
   - Earn enough experience for level 2 (a quest, or an encounter).
   - When no window is open, a **"Level 2 reached"** notice appears. It says what the level brings, how many choices it asks, and that nothing is taken until you confirm.
   - The HUD button turns bronze: **"Level 2 ready (L) · N choice(s)"**.
7. **Shown once.**
   - Click **Later**. The notice should not come back for the same level, even after saving and loading.
   - The button stays until you level.
8. **The overview.** Press **L**. The overview shows:
   - "Gained automatically when you confirm" (hit points, proficiency, features, resources);
   - "Your choices", each marked ✓ chosen, ◌ optional with nothing picked, or ○ still to choose;
   - "Still to come".
9. **Picks are kept.**
   - Make one choice, then close the window (Esc or Later).
   - The HUD tooltip says you started the level-up. Reopen it: your pick is still there and the overview says "Picking up where you left off".
   - Save, load, and reopen: still there.
10. **Nothing is chosen for you.** At level 3 (any class), the subclass page has nothing selected until you click one. Confirm stays disabled until every required choice is made.
11. **Confirming.**
    - Confirm. The level applies once: hit points rise, features appear, and the window moves on to the next level if you earned more.
    - After the last level, the sheet opens on **Progression**, with the class and subclass, "Latest: level N", the levels gained, and "Ahead: still locked" by level.
12. **No doubles.** Save and load after levelling. Level, hit points and the advancement list should be unchanged, with nothing gained twice.
13. **An older character.** Load a save made before Phase 22 that has a created tiefling. It still casts its legacy cantrip with Charisma. At its next level-up it is asked for the Legacy Spellcasting Ability.

## Goliath ancestry against spells

14. **Stone giant.** As a stone-giant goliath, fight an acolyte or another caster. When a spell (Sacred Flame) would hurt you, you are offered **Stone's Endurance**, which spends one ancestry use.
15. **Storm giant.** As a storm-giant goliath, a creature's spell that hurts you offers **Storm's Thunder** back at it.

## Phase 21 closeout (purchased kits)

16. **Seasons in the trees.** Visit a town in spring, summer, autumn and winter (or use the weather capture set):
    - broadleaf leaves fresh, as made, orange and gold, then dull and dusted with snow;
    - pines and cherry trees keep their colour.
17. **Trees by region.** Pines in a highland town, birches in a lakeland town.
18. **Doors and signs.** Every working door has the wooden door model across the whole doorway, with no gap. The smithy, provisions, apothecary and bowyer have signs outside.
19. **Furniture.** Homes have real beds (double, twin, bunk), nightstands, dressers and desks, and nothing blocks a doorway.
20. **A working shop and a station.**
    - Open a shop: its trade window shows stock, prices, shortfalls and the latest supply.
    - Use a station (the smithy's forge): crafting opens with "At the forge and anvil in …".
