# Phase 20A: Owner playtest checklist (lineages in play, eyes, familiarity)

**Automated vs manual.** The automated tests play every lineage benefit through the real combat engine, and the PlayMode test clicks the real creator and sheet. They cannot judge whether the fights feel right, whether the eyes look natural at your distance, or how the screens read at your resolution. Only you can.

**Set-up:**
1. Start a new world (Menu ▸ New world).
2. Enter a town.
3. Press **C**.

## A. The creator shows how each benefit is played
1. Choose **Goliath**:
   - [ ] Each trait and ancestry carries a badge: *active*, *conditional* or *not yet played*.
   - [ ] Each badge has words that say when it works.
2. Choose **Gnome**:
   - [ ] Forest and rock both say *not yet played*, and name the missing magic.
   - [ ] The rock gnome's tinker's tools still work.
3. Choose **Tiefling**:
   - [ ] Each legacy names its cantrip and says "cast with Charisma".
4. In **Appearance**, try every eye colour:
   - [ ] Only the iris changes; the whites and the skin around the eye do not.
   - [ ] Brown looks exactly as before.
5. Resize the window to 1366×768 and to 1280×720 (or set Interface scale 130 %):
   - [ ] The badges, swatches and descriptions do not clip or overlap.

## B. Goliath in a fight (create one per ancestry, or use the DM tools)
6. **Fire, frost or hill.** Hit a foe, then open **Features** in the combat window:
   - [ ] The boon is offered after the hit, and only then.
   - [ ] It spends one use (two at level 1).
   - [ ] Frost slows the foe; hill knocks it prone.
   - [ ] It is refused a second time on the same hit.
7. **Cloud.** Press **Choose a square**, then click a free square within 30 ft:
   - [ ] You reappear there.
   - [ ] No opportunity attack is made.
   - [ ] **Cancel** works.
   - [ ] Clicking the grid while aiming does not move you.
8. **Stone or storm.** Let a foe hit you:
   - [ ] You are asked whether to use the reaction.
   - [ ] Yes reduces the damage (stone) or thunders back (storm).
   - [ ] With no uses left, you are not asked.
9. **Rest.** Take a long rest:
   - [ ] The uses come back.

## C. Tiefling in a fight
10. As a tiefling **fighter**, open **Magic**:
    - [ ] Your legacy cantrip is listed as "innate cantrip (Charisma)".
    - [ ] Casting it rolls with your Charisma.
11. As a **chthonic** tiefling, hit with Chill Touch, then let the foe drink a potion or be healed:
    - [ ] It regains nothing until your next turn ends.

## D. Sheet
12. Press **H** ▸ Character ▸ **Origins**:
    - [ ] Each trait has its badge.
    - [ ] The words match what you saw in the fights.

## E. Look in the world
13. Create a character with a short height, a slight build and blue eyes. Then walk around the town:
    - [ ] The body in the world has the same height and build as the creator's preview. (This was broken before Phase 20A: the world always used average.)
    - [ ] The eyes are blue up close.

## F. Familiarity
14. Talk to the same person many times in one day:
    - [ ] "About them" does not reveal more after the first conversation that day.
15. Over about 25 in-game days of daily talk (or about 10 days of talk and trade with a keeper):
    - [ ] Their training and skills appear.
    - [ ] Is that pace right for you?

## G. Save and load
16. Save (F5), then Continue:
    - [ ] Eye colour, lineage and remaining ancestry uses are as you left them.
17. Load a world saved before Phase 20A:
    - [ ] Every character's eyes are brown, as they were.


## H. New assets (items, sounds, buildings)
18. Visit an armourer or general shop, and equip a sword, an axe, a spear, a bow and a shield:
    - [ ] Each is a modelled weapon, not a block shape, in the shop and in your hands.
    - [ ] Each is held the right way round and at a believable size (shield face outward, blade up).
    - [ ] Keys, potions, scrolls and books on shelves look like what they are.
19. Walk, run, enter a building, buy something, and open and close the character sheet (H):
    - [ ] You hear footsteps (faster when running), a door, coins and a page.
    - [ ] None is too loud or too frequent.
    - [ ] Settings ▸ Sound ▸ **Sound volume** changes them, and 0 is silent.
20. Look at the town's buildings:
    - [ ] Nothing looks different or broken compared with before. (The 103 new village pieces are available but not yet used by the building generator.)

**Report** anything misleading, with the step and the resolution.
