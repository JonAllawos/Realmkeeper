# Phase 19: owner playtest checklist (interface)

Automated captures are not human validation. These steps cover what only a person at the screen can judge. Each step takes a few minutes.

**Set-up.** Open `Assets/Realmkeeper/Scenes/Realmkeeper.unity` and press Play, so the title screen appears. If you can, also try a built player at 1366×768 or in a small window.

## A. Entry
1. The title shows over the living map. Read the menu.
   - [ ] Is every entry clear?
   - [ ] Does Continue name your newest world in its tooltip?
2. **New world:**
   - [ ] Clear the name: Found is disabled.
   - [ ] Type letters in the seed and press Found: a message appears and nothing happens.
   - [ ] Give a name and found the world: the map appears.
3. **Menu ▸ Load world:**
   - [ ] Your new world is listed with its date, seed and save time.
   - [ ] Delete it, then press **Cancel**: it stays.
   - [ ] Delete it and confirm: it goes.
4. **Menu ▸ Campaigns:**
   - [ ] Read *The Waystone Count* card.
   - [ ] Begin it, or note why you cannot.

## B. Settings
5. **Interface scale:**
   - [ ] Try 90 %, 130 % and Auto. Text and panels scale together everywhere.
   - [ ] Is 130 % comfortable on your display?
6. **Display:**
   - [ ] In a built player, change Display mode and Resolution, and check they apply.
   - [ ] The controls list matches what the keys do.

## C. Strategic map and atlas
7. **Readability:**
   - [ ] Hover the Play, Politics and Speed controls: tooltips appear after about half a second.
   - [ ] Select a settlement: the inspector's sections each have an icon.
8. **Load:**
   - [ ] Press Load: a dialog warns that progress since the save is lost.
   - [ ] Cancel it, and check the world is unchanged.
9. **Atlas (A, or the World atlas button):**
   - [ ] Is the parchment map readable at a glance?
   - [ ] Switch layers off and on.
   - [ ] Unknown regions look like blank, uncharted map, with no detail you have not learned.

## D. In a settlement
10. **HUD:**
    - [ ] Walk around. Is the top-left strip (time, Hub, Party) clear?
    - [ ] Are the practice and outing buttons quiet enough?
11. **Hub (H):**
    - [ ] Go through all 13 sections. The icons help you scan.
    - [ ] Nothing overlaps or clips at your resolution.
12. **Dialogue:**
    - [ ] The speaker's name and spoken line read well in the serif face.
    - [ ] Checks show their skill and DC.
13. **Combat (F, a practice fight):**
    - [ ] Action, Bonus and Reaction each show an icon. After using your action, it dims and turns italic.
    - [ ] The battlefield stays visible.

## E. Report back
- [ ] Anything that looks wrong, misleading or hard to read, with the screen and the resolution.
- [ ] Does the overall look feel like one game, warm and grounded, without being over-decorated?
