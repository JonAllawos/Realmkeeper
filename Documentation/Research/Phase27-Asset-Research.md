# Phase 27: asset research for children, factions, building types, stalls and signs

**Date:** 2026-11-14.

**Asked by the owner:** research CC0 or CC BY assets for children, factions, building types, a smithy that is partly outdoors, outdoor stall shops and signs, and for anything earlier phases still need.

**Rule applied:** owned and free CC0 first; paid assets only with the owner's authorisation. **Nothing was bought or downloaded this round.**
