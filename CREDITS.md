The SRD supplies the rules mechanics:
- species, classes and progression;
- armour, weapons, conditions and combat;
- spells (levels, ranges, areas, damage, saves, durations) and spellcasting;
- class features in combat, rests and recovery;
- the bandit, guard, commoner and wolf statistics (SRD 5.2.1), and the acolyte and cult fanatic
  statistics with their spellcasting (SRD 5.1);
- the equipment and magic items added in Phase 11, from SRD 5.2.1: the simple and martial weapons'
  damage, properties and mastery; the armour table; ammunition; artisan's tools; the potion of healing
  and greater healing; +1 weapons, armour and shield; the ring and cloak of protection; gauntlets of ogre
  power; goggles of night; boots of elvenkind; the wand of magic missiles; the Berserker Axe and its curse
  (as rules: bonuses, attunement, charges and recharge); carrying capacity and the attunement limit.
  Item descriptions and lore are Realmkeeper's own;
- the bestiary added in Phase 10, from SRD 5.1: deer, boar, black bear, brown bear, dire wolf, giant rat,
  giant spider, goat, riding horse, draft horse, mastiff, goblin, thug (named "highwayman" in the game),
  bandit captain, cultist, veteran, skeleton, zombie, ogre and troll, with their traits (Charge,
  Regeneration, Undead Fortitude, Relentless, Nimble Escape, Parry and others).

**Species lineages (Phase 20).** The tiefling's fiendish legacies (abyssal, chthonic, infernal), the goliath's giant
ancestries (cloud, fire, frost, hill, stone, storm) and the gnome's lineages (forest, rock) follow SRD 5.2.1 as rules; their
descriptions are Realmkeeper's own.

**Realmkeeper originals (not SRD).** The rabbit, fox, cattle, sheep and chicken have no SRD statistics.
Their statistics are Realmkeeper's own, written in the same format and labelled
`ContentSource.RealmkeeperOriginal` in the catalog and in the game's combat inspector. Creature
descriptions, behaviour summaries, settlement identities, district and landmark names, and the NPC
archetypes are Realmkeeper's own.

**Quests and dialogue (Phase 12).** Every quest, objective, dialogue line, rumour and reputation text is
Realmkeeper's own, written for this project. No proprietary dialogue or setting content is used, and no
campaign canon (kingdoms, the protagonist, a central antagonist) is established. Skill checks in
conversation use the SRD 5.2.1 skills already credited above.

**Places (Phase 13).** Every place name, description, clue, document, trap and hazard text, and the rescue quest, is Realmkeeper's own, written for this project. Archetypes are generic kinds (mine, crypt, cave and so on), not any game's levels. Place geometry, props and figures are built at runtime from Unity primitives and generated meshes: no new art assets, no third-party models. Traps, hazards and checks use the SRD 5.2.1 rules already credited (skills, saves, damage types). No campaign canon is established.

**Places, crime and conditions (Phase 14).** The five new places, their stories, quests and dialogue, the crime, condition and rest texts are Realmkeeper's own, written for this project. Line of sight and cover follow the SRD 5.2.1 cover rules (half, three-quarters, total) with Realmkeeper's own grid method; conditions, exhaustion and Lesser Restoration are the SRD 5.2.1 rules already credited. The new places are dressed with the CC0 kits and materials already listed below (Quaternius, ambientCG): no new third-party assets were added.

Spell names are the SRD's; every spell and feature description, and all names and setting text,
are Realmkeeper's own. Realmkeeper does not use the
"Dungeons & Dragons" trademark and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast. Which
content comes from which SRD is listed per definition (`ContentSource`) and in
`Documentation/Systems/Characters.md`, `Documentation/Systems/Combat.md` and
`Documentation/Systems/SpellCatalogAudit.md`.
